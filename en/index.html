How I can help

Architecture that connects your business and your technology.

  • Solution architecture

    You have a goal, and I help shape the system that gets you there. We weigh performance, scalability and maintainability together, so the design still makes sense a few years from now.

  • Architecture reviews & advice

    Stuck on a choice, or want a second pair of eyes? I review your architecture and strategy with you and talk through the trade-offs in plain language, so you can decide with confidence.

  • Clear decisions & documentation

    Diagrams, decision records and a well-kept application landscape. Everybody understands what we build, and why.

  • Hands-on when it counts

    When it helps, I join the team in the code and the pipelines to turn the design into working software.

How I work

Calm, open-minded and curious.

I'm interested in a lot of things, and solving problems comes naturally to me. I use a mix of pragmatism and experimentation: try something, learn from it, improve.

The people I work with, on projects and off them, are what keeps me getting better every day. What I bring is the overview and the details together: seeing how the business fits together, then making it work in code.

  1. Listen first

    Every good design starts with understanding what you really need, not with a technology.

  2. Keep it simple

    Pick the simplest thing that works, and write down why so the reasoning lasts.

  3. Stay hands-on

    I don't hand over diagrams and disappear. I help build it, too.
